None of that would be possible without the patches to chromium as you cannot capture controlled frames and render to canvas, webgl, or webgpu. The patches made it possible and then the ui shown in the video is just what you can do once you render to webgl / webgpu. When pages are interactive or full screen they are actually overlayed controlled frames. It snaps between the two as you use the ui.
