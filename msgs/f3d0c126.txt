In our [interactive demo](https://dialog-db.github.io/wasm-component-model-polyfill/) you can (re)compile Zena programs - a language designed to compile directly to Wasm - to Wasm components right there in the tab. The Wasm components, in turn, get compiled, linked and instantiated by the polyfill. These components could run unmodified in Wasmtime, assuming you brought the expected imports. Those expected imports could be satisfed by host APIs or other Wasm component guests at link time.
